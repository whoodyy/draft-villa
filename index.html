Questions

Frequently asked

What is included in the stay?

Free Wi-Fi, free access to the non-heated swimming pool, basic kitchen and dining utensils, an electric kettle, a microwave, a rice cooker, an Android TV, and a mini karaoke set. Each loft has a mini kitchen and a bathroom with a heater.

How many guests can stay?

Each loft sleeps six. Three lofts host up to 18 guests. Extra guests beyond 18 pay P300 per head.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 noon.

Is the stay exclusive to my group?

Yes. Your booking is 21 hours of exclusive use of the property.

Can I cook during my stay?

Yes. Basic kitchen utensils are free to use. For heavy cooking, use the open kitchen stove, which has a P200 gas charge. You can also use the BBQ grill. Please bring your own charcoal.

Is parking available?

Yes. Parking is free for up to five cars.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome. Please be a responsible pet owner. Diapers are provided at all times.

Is the pool heated?

No, the swimming pool is non-heated. Access is free for guests.

How do I book?

Call or text us at 0915 131 9701 to check availability and reserve your dates.
